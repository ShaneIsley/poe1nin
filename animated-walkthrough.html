Strategy 4

Market Movers Analysis

Every analysis run computes % change = (current − previous) / previous × 100 for each item, filters out low-value noise, and surfaces the biggest gainers and losers. Adjust volatility to simulate different market conditions.

# analysis.py lines 103–107
df_movers = df[ df['imputed_chaos_value'] > 10 ]
df_movers['change'] = ((current - previous) / previous) * 100
top_gainers = df_movers.sort_values('change').tail(10)
top_losers  = df_movers.sort_values('change').head(10)
